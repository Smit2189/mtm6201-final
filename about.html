About

Plant Palette began as a dream to create a vibrant space where people could enjoy delicious, plant-based food while being educated about the health benefits of a vegan lifestyle. After years of working in the culinary industry, I, Tom (the owner), felt a strong pull to start a restaurant that not only serves meals but also fosters community connections centered around healthy eating and environmental awareness.

From the beginning, the goal was to create a welcoming atmosphere where diners can not only indulge in amazing food but also learn about the ingredients they’re consuming. We dedicate time to train our staff not just in culinary skills, but also in the philosophy of our restaurant. This rich history and commitment to our values is something we want to showcase on our website, complete with stories about our farmers, customer experiences, and the inspiration behind our dishes.

At Plant Palette, we prioritize sustainability and support our local farming community. All our ingredients are sourced from reputable local farms that practice organic and ethical agricultural methods. We believe in transparency and connecting our customers with the source of their food, creating an enriching dining experience that celebrates the bounty of our region.
